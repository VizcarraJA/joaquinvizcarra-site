# LinkedIn — WPC 2026 Session Announcement

---

The countdown has officially begun. 🧠

In five weeks, Phoenix hosts the World Parkinson Congress — and I am thrilled to be part of one of the sessions I think will generate some of the most important conversations of the congress.

**MCS2 — Artificial Intelligence, Big Data, and Digital Devices in Parkinson's Care**

Here is what we have lined up:

🎙 **Moderator: Diane Stephenson (USA)**
🎙 **Co-Moderator: Camille Carroll (UK)**

Two leaders who know how to build a session that actually goes somewhere — I could not ask for better people steering the room.

**Talk 1 — Harnessing AI: Revolutionizing Parkinson's Clinical Care**
**Bill Marks (USA)**
The implementation question. Not whether AI can perform a task in a controlled study, but what it looks like when it actually enters the clinic and changes the way clinicians work.

**Talk 2 — From Big Data to Personalized Care**
**Joaquín Vizcarra (USA)** ← that's me
The translation problem. The field has data at unprecedented scale. What it does not yet have is a reliable architecture for moving from population-level patterns to decisions that are actually useful for an individual patient sitting in front of you. That is the gap I want to spend twenty minutes on in Phoenix.

**Talk 3 — Digital Endpoints in Clinical Research: Are We There Yet?**
**Anat Mirelman (Israel)**
An honest reckoning with where wearable-generated endpoints have delivered in clinical trials, where the evidence is still developing, and what needs to happen before digital measurement earns the same confidence as traditional outcomes.

---

Three talks. One throughline: the distance between what the field knows and what actually reaches patients — and what closing that distance is going to require.

I am genuinely excited about this session. Diane, Camille, Bill, and Anat are all people I learn from, and the combination of implementation, translation, and measurement in a single ninety-minute block is exactly the kind of integrated conversation the field needs more of.

If you are coming to WPC Phoenix, come find us. If you are not — I will be writing about it.

See you in Phoenix. 🌵
